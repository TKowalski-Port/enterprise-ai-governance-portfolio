Case Study

Governance Workflow Automation & Process Transformation

This case study reflects governance consulting work that established the policies, standards, workflows, and controls needed to prepare an organization for responsible AI adoption. It serves as a foundation before implementing AI governance, as illustrated in the Enterprise AI & GRC Portfolio.

Problem

Manual, disconnected governance processes left leadership without clear visibility into risk, slowed decisions, increased project costs, and increased audit exposure.

What Was Built

  • Structured workflow connecting ownership, approvals, and decisions.
  • Defined roles, responsibilities, and decision points.
  • Automated evidence collection, reducing administrative effort.
  • Reporting mechanisms to improve visibility and support informed decision-making.
  • Exception monitoring to identify risk.

Business Impact

Board

Increased confidence in enterprise risk posture through improved visibility into governance activities, decision outcomes, and emerging risk exposure.

Executive Leadership

Enabled faster, consistent, and risk-informed decisions through clear accountability, defined ownership, and standardized governance processes.

Governance Committee

Reduced decision ambiguity by establishing repeatable evaluation criteria, escalation paths, exception management, and audit-ready evidence.

Operations

Reduced manual coordination effort and improved execution efficiency through standardized workflows, automated evidence collection, and lifecycle visibility.
